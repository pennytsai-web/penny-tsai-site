The funding

Up to 70% funded for SMEs.

Under the SkillsFuture Workforce Development Grant (Job Redesign+), eligible Singapore SMEs get up to 70% funding support, capped at $150,000 per enterprise. Non-SMEs get up to 50%. As a WSG Pre-Approved consultant, Elitez guides you through the whole application.

  • Registered or incorporated and operating in Singapore
  • At least 3 local employees (Singapore Citizens or PRs)
  • Have not previously signed a job redesign consultancy contract
